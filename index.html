THE DOLPHIN TABLE · SINCE 1985

Feeding Vijayawada for 40 years.

For four decades, Dolphin's Family Restaurant has been serving the city's favourite biryanis, curries and tandoor plates — the same recipes, the same welcome, three generations of diners.

From the first bowl of soup to the last spoon of biryani, we cook for the way Vijayawada eats: together, with plenty on the table and no need to hurry.
